# Operational Efficiency

Why teams, codebases, and orgs slow down over time, and where the actual leverage is — as opposed to where it looks like it should be.

- **[Systems Thinking](../frameworks/systems-thinking/Systems%20Thinking%20-%20Agent-written.md)** — the underlying theory: [Feedback Loops](../frameworks/systems-thinking/concepts/feedback-loops.md), [Stocks & Flows](../frameworks/systems-thinking/concepts/stocks-and-flows.md), [Delays](../frameworks/systems-thinking/concepts/delays.md), [Hierarchy & Suboptimization](../frameworks/systems-thinking/concepts/hierarchy.md), [Resilience](../frameworks/systems-thinking/concepts/resilience.md), [System Limits](../frameworks/systems-thinking/concepts/limits.md), [System Traps](../frameworks/systems-thinking/concepts/system-traps.md).

## Situations

- **[Velocity Decline](../frameworks/systems-thinking/problems/velocity-decline.md)** — throughput dropping despite stable headcount and effort.
- **[Architecture Debt](../frameworks/systems-thinking/problems/architecture-debt.md)** — every change slower and riskier than the last.
- **[Retention Problem](../frameworks/systems-thinking/problems/retention.md)** — experienced people leaving faster than they can be replaced.
- **[Onboarding Friction](../frameworks/systems-thinking/problems/onboarding-friction.md)** — new hires taking 3+ months to become productive.
- **[Cross-Team Communication](../frameworks/systems-thinking/problems/cross-team-communication.md)** — misalignment, duplicate work, and blocked dependencies between teams.
