# Topics

Frameworks, tools, and people are organized by *kind* under `frameworks/`, `tools/`, and `people/`. This index organizes the same material by *theme*, so you can find everything relevant to a situation regardless of which folder it lives in.

**How to use:** Find the theme closest to what you're working on, then follow its links. This page and the theme pages don't duplicate content — they only point to it.

## Themes

- **[Product Management](product-management.md)** — deciding what to build and why.
- **[Processes & Rituals](processes-and-rituals.md)** — recurring team mechanisms: how work gets proposed, chosen, and paced.
- **[Operational Efficiency](operational-efficiency.md)** — why teams, codebases, and orgs slow down, and where the actual leverage is.
- **[Thinking & Communication](thinking-and-communication.md)** — tools for asking a better question rather than just giving a faster answer.
- **[Career & Negotiation](career-and-negotiation.md)** — advocating for yourself in comp and leveling conversations.

## For Agents

Every agent-written framework and tool landing page carries a `topics` field in frontmatter, listing which of these themes it belongs to — that's the reverse lookup. Human-written source pages (most of `tools/` and `people/`) are never edited to add this field, per [METHODOLOGY.md](../docs/METHODOLOGY.md); their topic assignment lives here instead, one-directionally, until they're broken down and get an agent-written landing page of their own.
