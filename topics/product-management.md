# Product Management

Frameworks and tools for deciding what to build and why, and people whose thinking shapes how to do that well.

## Frameworks

- **[Shape Up](../frameworks/shape-up/Shape%20Up%20-%20Agent-written.md)** — bounding work by time instead of scope, so projects converge instead of running forever.

## Tools

- **[Opportunity Solution Trees](../tools/opportunity-solution-trees.md)** — Teresa Torres' structure for connecting a desired outcome to the opportunities and solutions that could move it, so discovery stays traceable back to outcomes.

## People

- **[Shishir Mehrotra](../people/shishir-mehrotra.md)** — planning frameworks (Eigenquestions, $100 Bets) and the bundling/unbundling lens on product strategy. Also the vault's one thread on AI-native culture — see [Coverage Gaps](Topics.md#coverage-gaps).
