# Cool-down

## When to Use

Use this immediately after a build cycle ends. Cool-down is the two-week gap before the next cycle starts — unscheduled time, by design, rather than a gap that quietly fills with whatever's next.

Reach for this specifically when someone's proposing to schedule work into that gap, or skip it to start the next cycle sooner — that's the anti-pattern this ritual exists to prevent.

## How to Act

**Ask first:**
- Is there pressure to fill this gap with scheduled work? Where's that pressure coming from?
- Is there tech debt or exploration that's been deferred cycle after cycle because there's "never time"?

**Where I'd start:** Keep cool-down genuinely unscheduled. People are free to work on tech debt, explore ideas, or look into new technical possibilities — but not because it's assigned; because it's the only structurally-protected time that isn't claimed by a bet. This is also when the [Betting](betting.md) table happens for the next cycle, since everyone's not mid-build.

**What would change this:** If cool-down keeps getting quietly filled with "just this one thing" from the previous cycle, that's a sign the previous cycle's scope wasn't actually cut to fit six weeks — the fix is tighter [Shaping](shaping.md), not sacrificing the gap.
