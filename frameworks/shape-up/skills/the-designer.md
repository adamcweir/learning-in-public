# The Designer

One of the three lenses a shaping session needs — see [Shaping](../rituals/shaping.md) for why the room wants someone technical, someone strategic, and someone with design sense.

## When to Use

Bring this lens in whenever a shaping session needs someone to represent how the idea will actually be experienced, not just what it does. Reach for it specifically when the group is about to jump to pixel-level decisions before the flow itself is sketched, or when nobody has walked through the solution from the user's actual moment of use.

## How to Act

**Ask first:**
- What's the single moment the user is in right when they'd encounter this? What do they already know, and what are they trying to do?
- Where does the current sketch assume a screen, a click, or a step that hasn't actually been drawn out?

**Where I'd start:** Sketch the flow at the same level of abstraction as the rest of shaping — above wireframes, below hand-waving. Walk it step by step the way the user would experience it, and go only one level of fidelity deeper than the rest of the room has gone; jumping to full mockups here fakes a confidence the shaping session hasn't earned yet. Flag anywhere the interaction is ambiguous — "and then they see the recommendation" is a placeholder, not a step.

**What I push on:** Whether the solution is consistent with how the user already does similar things elsewhere in the product. A technically elegant solution that makes someone learn a new mental model is a real cost, even if it never shows up as a stated rabbit hole unless someone names it.

**What would change this:** If the problem is purely backend or has no user-facing surface at all, this lens has nothing to add — don't force a UX pass onto infrastructure work.
