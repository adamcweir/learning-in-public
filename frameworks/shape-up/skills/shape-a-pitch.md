# Shape a Pitch

The meta-skill: run [Shaping](../rituals/shaping.md) as a simulated three-person session — [The Designer](the-designer.md), [The Strategist](the-strategist.md), [The Technical Implementer](the-technical-implementer.md) — and come out the other side with a filled-in [pitch](../rituals/pitch.md).

## When to Use

Use this when someone brings a raw idea or problem and wants a pitch, but doesn't have an actual room of collaborators to shape it with — you stand in for all three lenses in sequence.

## How to Act

Run the three lenses in this order, not all at once — each depends on what the last one settled:

1. **[The Strategist](the-strategist.md) first.** Fix the problem statement and the appetite before anything is sketched. If the appetite doesn't survive this pass, stop here — there's no pitch yet, there's a decision about whether the idea is worth shaping at all.
2. **[The Designer](the-designer.md) and [The Technical Implementer](the-technical-implementer.md) together, in conversation.** Rough out the solution as a flow, and pressure-test it for buildability at the same time — these two lenses correct each other in real time, which is why the source material puts them in the same room rather than in sequence.
3. **Back to [The Strategist](the-strategist.md).** Check the roughed-out solution still fits the appetite fixed in step 1. If it's grown past it, cut scope now, not during building.

**Where I'd start:** Don't run all three as a single pass and hope it converges — the strategist has to bound the problem before the other two lenses have anything concrete to react to, and has to close the loop again afterward, or scope quietly grows to fill whatever the designer and implementer found interesting.

Write the result into the [pitch template](../artifacts/shape-up-pitch-template.md): the strategist pass becomes the problem statement, appetite, and no-gos; the designer/implementer pass becomes the solution sketch and rabbit holes.

**What would change this:** If the idea is already well-scoped and low-risk, running all three personas is overkill — see [Shaping](../rituals/shaping.md)'s own note on when the full ritual isn't needed. A known bug doesn't need a strategist pass.
